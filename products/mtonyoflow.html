MtonyoFlow

Mfumo wa Usimamizi wa Fedha kwa Biashara Ndogo za Tanzania

(Financial Management System for Small Tanzanian Businesses)

What Is MtonyoFlow?

MtonyoFlow is a multi-channel financial management platform built exclusively for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) in Tanzania. It is not a copy of a foreign accounting tool adapted for Africa. It is not a generic bookkeeping app translated into Swahili. It is a system designed from the ground up for the Tanzanian business owner.

Three-Channel Integration

MtonyoFlow works across three channels simultaneously:

  • Web Application (PWA) — for smartphone users with internet access
  • SMS — for basic phone users who send commands like SALE 5000 or EXPENSE 2000
  • USSD — for anyone on any phone, on any network, with zero internet required

All three channels feed into one unified system. One database. One balance. One complete financial picture — regardless of which channel the business owner uses to record their transactions.

The Problem MtonyoFlow Solves

Tanzania has over four million SMEs contributing more than 35 percent of the national GDP. They employ millions of Tanzanians and form the backbone of the economy. Yet the overwhelming majority of them are running their businesses completely blind.

1. Manual Bookkeeping

Most SME owners record their income and expenses in exercise books, on scraps of paper, or in their memory. There is no aggregation, no calculation, no analysis. At the end of the week, they count cash and guess whether they made a profit.

2. No Real-Time Financial Visibility

A business owner in Tanzania cannot answer simple questions like:

  • How much money do I have right now?
  • How much did I spend today?
  • Did I make a profit this week?
  • Will I have enough money next month?

3. Unexpected Cash Shortages

The most common cause of SME failure in Sub-Saharan Africa is not bad products or lazy owners. It is cash flow mismanagement. Money runs out without warning. Rent cannot be paid. Stock cannot be replenished.

4. TRA Tax Penalties

The Tanzania Revenue Authority (TRA) requires businesses to file annual tax returns. Without proper records, this becomes a crisis every January.

5. No Access to Financial Tools

The tools that exist for financial management were built for large enterprises in developed economies. They are expensive, complex, require constant internet, and are entirely uncalibrated to the realities of a duka owner.

MtonyoFlow closes every one of these gaps.

Core Features of MtonyoFlow

1. Real-Time Balance Tracking

Every transaction recorded — whether via the web app, SMS command, or USSD menu — instantly updates the user's running balance.

2. Three-Channel Transaction Entry

Record a sale by opening the web app, sending SALE 5000 as SMS, or dialling a USSD code. All three paths save to the same database.

3. Automated Alerts

The system sends SMS warnings when:

  • Cash balance falls below TZS 10,000
  • Daily expenses exceed TZS 50,000
  • No income recorded for 24 hours

4. AI-Powered 30-Day Cash Flow Forecast

Using a Moving Average model, MtonyoFlow predicts the next 30 days of cash flow. Each predicted day is classified as CRITICAL, LOW RISK, or HEALTHY.

5. Financial Reports

  • Weekly Summary — income, expenses, profit
  • Monthly P&L — profit & loss statement
  • TRA Annual Report — Tanzania Revenue Authority compliant
  • Budget Report — AI-powered recommendations
  • Invoices — professional invoices with VAT

6. Offline-First Design

MtonyoFlow works without internet. The web app caches offline. Transactions entered without connectivity sync automatically when internet returns.

7. TRA Tax Compliance

The system automatically calculates Tanzania's presumptive tax brackets and generates a compliant annual tax report.

8. Mobile Money Payments

Pay for your subscription via M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Mixx by Yas, or HaloPesa.
