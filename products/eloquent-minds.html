THE ELOQUENT MINDS

SPEAK. LEAD. INSPIRE.

A professional public speaking and leadership development community dedicated to empowering the next generation of visionary leaders.

What Is The Eloquent Minds?

The Eloquent Minds is Yusuphu Awadhi Lema's initiative to empower the next generation of leaders through the art of public speaking, leadership development, and executive presence.

Modeled after the Toastmasters International framework, The Eloquent Minds is a professional community where members practice public speaking, develop leadership skills, and build confidence in high-pressure environments.

We believe that the best leaders are excellent communicators. The ability to articulate vision, inspire teams, and navigate complex conversations is not innate—it is a craft that can be learned, practiced, and mastered.

The Difference We Make:

Most leadership programs teach theory in a classroom. The Eloquent Minds teaches leadership in practice. You learn by doing, by speaking, by failing safely, and by receiving real feedback from peers who understand your goals.

Why The Eloquent Minds Matters

1. Most Leaders Never Learn to Speak

In East Africa, we have brilliant technologists, entrepreneurs, and visionaries. But many of them communicate their ideas poorly. A world-class idea communicated badly will fail. The Eloquent Minds changes this.

2. Confidence is Competitive Advantage

In business, fundraising, and public influence, the person who speaks with clarity and confidence often wins. This is not about being loud or performative. It is about being clear, composed, and commanding.

3. Communication Shapes the World

Every major movement—political, social, technological—is shaped by people who can speak. If we want Tanzania and Africa to shape its own future, we need leaders who can articulate that future powerfully.

4. It's Part of Lemanyx Intelligence

The Eloquent Minds is not separate from Lemanyx Intelligence. It IS Lemanyx Intelligence. Our philosophy is simple: technology without communication fails. Solutions without advocates go unused. Excellence without eloquence goes unnoticed.

Our Three Pillars

Every member of The Eloquent Minds develops excellence across three dimensions:

1. Oratory

Master the techniques of powerful speaking: voice, pacing, pauses, body language, and persuasion. Learn to captivate an audience and communicate complex ideas with clarity.

2. Leadership

Develop the vision, strategic thinking, and emotional intelligence that great leaders possess. Learn to inspire teams, navigate crisis, and drive organizational excellence.

3. Executive Presence

Command respect in the boardroom. Develop the composure, professionalism, and gravitas that separates leaders from managers.

How We Meet & Learn

Frequency & Schedule

When:

Every 2 weeks • In-person + Online

Where:

Dar es Salaam, Tanzania (+ Virtual access)

Duration:

2-3 hours per session

Cost:

Affordable membership • First meeting FREE

What Happens in a Session

  • Prepared Speeches: Members deliver 5-7 minute speeches on topics of their choice. Full feedback from the audience.
  • Impromptu Speaking: Practice thinking on your feet with 1-2 minute impromptu challenges.
  • Table Topics: Interactive discussions on leadership, communication, and current events.
  • Evaluations: Receive constructive, professional feedback on your delivery from experienced speakers.
  • Networking: Connect with other leaders, entrepreneurs, and professionals building their craft.
